JG-LGL-003 · MARKETPLACE VENDOR AGREEMENT (बजार विक्रेता सम्झौता)
Agreement · v1.0 · Updated 12 Apr 2026
Applies to: Product brands, wholesalers, B2B salon suppliers
========================================================================

This Agreement is entered into between Junetara Glam Pvt. Ltd. (the
"Company") and the product vendor identified in the execution block
(the "Vendor"), effective as of the date of digital execution.

1. SCOPE
The Vendor lists products for retail (B2C) and wholesale (B2B) sale on
the marketplace, including salon supplies and equipment.

2. LISTING STANDARDS
Descriptions must be accurate and complete, including expiry dates and
MRP. Counterfeit goods, unlicensed imports and expired stock are
prohibited and lead to immediate de-listing.

3. COMMISSION & SUBSCRIPTION
Retail: 5-10% by category. Wholesale B2B: 3-5%. Vendors may subscribe
at NPR 1,000-5,000/month for reduced commission tiers and priority
placement.

4. FULFILLMENT & DELIVERY
Kathmandu Valley orders ship within 48 hours; national orders via
partner couriers with tracking shared to the platform.

5. RETURNS & WARRANTY
Customers receive 7-day returns; defective items are the Vendor's cost.
Refunds issue through the original payment gateway within 48 hours to
7 days.

6. SETTLEMENT
Weekly settlement cycle, T+7 days. Chargebacks and verified refunds are
deducted from the next payout with itemised statements.

7. BRAND & IP
The Vendor warrants it holds rights to sell all listed brands and
grants the Company a license to use product imagery on the platform.

8. DATA SHARING
Demand analytics are shared with the Vendor; the Vendor shall not
harvest customer contact data for off-platform marketing.

9. TERM & DISPUTES
12-month auto-renewing term; 30 days' notice to terminate; NEPCA
arbitration, Kathmandu; laws of Nepal govern.
